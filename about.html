About Us

Our company history and growth journey.

History of the Company

Founded in 2008, Hollywood Advertising - Art Production started as a boutique creative agency focused on visual storytelling for local businesses.

Between 2012 and 2018, the company expanded into large-format out-of-home advertising, securing landmark billboard locations and launching a production division for ad films.

Since 2019, we have delivered integrated campaigns that combine billboard strategy, TV production, and digital amplification for regional and national brands.
